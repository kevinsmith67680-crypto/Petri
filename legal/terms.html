4. How a round works

  • A round begins once enough players have marked themselves ready, and runs for a fixed time — ten minutes by default.
  • You choose a stake before joining. That amount is taken from your balance and held in escrow for the round.
  • Practice is free, runs in your own browser against bots, and never involves money.

5. How stakes are settled

These are the rules the server actually applies:

You are eaten by another staked playerYour escrow transfers to them. You lose your stake.
You are eaten by a bot, or by a player with nothing at riskYour stake is forfeited. Nobody wins it.
You survive to the whistle and place in the paid positionsYour escrow is paid out to your balance, less any rake.
You survive to the whistle but place outside themYour stake is forfeited.
You disconnect mid-roundYour cells remain in the arena, motionless and edible, for a short period. If another player eats them, they take your stake exactly as if you were still playing. If they survive that period unclaimed, your stake is refunded.

Two consequences worth stating plainly. There is no voluntary cash-out. Once a round starts, the only way to realise your stake is to be alive and placed when the round ends. And disconnecting is not an escape: your cells stay on the board and can be taken, which is why quitting a losing fight does not protect your stake.

Only the top finishers are paid. The number of paid positions is shown on the pregame menu for each mode — currently the top five.

6. The cash figure shown during play

The game displays an indicative cash value next to your mass. It is a display convenience only. Settlement is never calculated from it. What you are paid depends on your finishing position and the stake held in escrow, not on the number on screen.

7. Deposits, withdrawals and rake

Deposits and withdrawals are handled in USDC. Blockchain transactions are irreversible and we cannot recover funds sent to a wrong address. Network fees are yours.

We may take a percentage of winnings as rake. Where a rake applies, the rate will be stated before you stake. The deposit and withdrawal process, minimum amounts, processing times and any limits are still to be specified and must be documented here before real money is enabled.

8. Fair play and the authoritative server

The server owns the only real copy of the world. Your client sends an aim direction and a few discrete actions; everything else — where your cell is, what it has eaten, how much mass it has — is decided by the server. Speed hacks, teleporting and editing your own mass are impossible by design rather than by detection.

Latency varies between players and is not something we can equalise. By playing you accept that network conditions affect play and are not grounds for a refund.
